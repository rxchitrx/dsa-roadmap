## Design Context

### Users

This is a personal DSA learning workspace used during focused desktop study sessions. The primary job is to turn a weekly DSA routine into an actionable loop: learn a concept, solve problems, revisit older problems, reflect on mistakes, and plan the next week.

### Brand Personality

Calm, focused, confidence-building. The interface should feel like a serious study desk rather than a game, leaderboard, or developer dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark throughout, with deep ink surfaces and warm amber focus states. Use restrained semantic colors for outcomes. Favor a quiet, deliberate workspace with strong hierarchy and useful density; avoid neon, gamified reward mechanics, and generic analytics-card layouts.

### Design Principles

- Put the next useful action at the center of the Today view.
- Keep planning, solving, reflection, and analytics visually distinct.
- Show evidence behind recommendations and progress claims.
- Preserve focus during long coding sessions with low-glare surfaces and minimal distraction.
- Prefer meaningful feedback over decorative metrics.
